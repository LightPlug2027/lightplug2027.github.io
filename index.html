CVPR 2027 · Face Relight

LightPlug: Bringing out Facial Structure in Avatar Relighting via a Lightweight Plug-and-Play Prior

LightPlug is a lightweight plug-and-play module, which enables cast-shadow-aware face relighting and adjust for every codec avatars.

[ 01 ]

Abstract

Realistic avatar relighting must reproduce local shading variations and cast shadows around facial structures to convey facial depth. However, methods trained on in-the-wild images often lack sufficient observations under varied illumination to recover these responses, resulting in overly smooth facial shading. Although one-light-at-a-time (OLAT) captures provide measured illumination responses, their acquisition is costly, highlighting the need for a lightweight, reusable prior for existing avatar renderers. We introduce LightPlug, a lightweight, plug-and-play module that transfers structure-dependent responses learned from OLAT observations across avatar representations. Conditioned on facial albedo and a light embedding, LightPlug learns local illumination responses in canonical UV space, where corresponding facial regions are aligned across observations. During deployment, a selected response is converted into a relative spatial prior, aligned with the renderer's shading locations through a framework-specific mapping, and used to multiplicatively modulate its native diffuse response under the target illumination. This modular design enables reuse across avatar representations while preserving the base renderer's target illumination computation, geometry, material parameters, and specular branch. Experiments on multiple facial datasets, avatar frameworks, and unseen identities demonstrate improved local shading around facial structures and the applicability of the learned prior across rendering pipelines. With approximately 1.12 ms per prior inference, LightPlug provides an efficient approach to enhancing structure-dependent facial shading in existing avatar relighting pipelines.
